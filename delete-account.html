Delete your Chef Taste account

You can delete your Chef Taste account and personal data at any time.

IN THE APP (FASTEST)

  1. Open the Chef Taste app and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Scroll to Legal & Account and tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm. Your account is deleted immediately and you are signed out.

WITHOUT THE APP

Email CONTACT_EMAIL from the address you registered with, with the subject "Delete my account". We confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days, usually much sooner.

WHAT IS DELETED

  • Your name, email address, phone number and login.
  • Saved addresses, map pins, dietary preferences and allergies.
  • For chefs: your CV, availability and menus, which are removed from the platform, and the bank details you gave us for payouts.
  • Your saved payment profile with our payment provider, Stripe.

WHAT IS KEPT, AND WHY

Records of past bookings and payments are kept in anonymised form, without your name or contact details, for as long as tax and accounting law requires (generally 6 years). Messages and photos exchanged about a past booking stay attached to that anonymised record.

BEFORE YOU DELETE

Bookings that are still in progress (requested, awaiting payment, confirmed, awaiting your confirmation or under dispute) must be completed or cancelled first. Chefs must also have received their final payout. The app tells you if anything is still open.
