3. HOW BOOKINGS WORK

  • You submit a request for a menu, a number of guests, a date, a time and an address. Bookings must be requested at least 24 hours in advance.
  • The Chef has 72 hours to accept or decline. Requests not answered in time expire automatically.
  • If a menu requires a deposit, you have 24 hours after acceptance to pay it. The booking is confirmed when the deposit is paid; if it is not paid in time, the booking expires. Menus without a deposit are confirmed on acceptance.
  • The balance (the total price minus any deposit) is paid in the App no later than 48 hours before the event. If your booking is confirmed less than 48 hours before the event, the balance is due straight away. We remind you before it is due. If it remains unpaid, we may cancel the booking; the deposit is then not refunded.
  • After the event, you confirm the experience in the App or report a problem within the confirmation window shown on the menu (48 hours unless the menu states otherwise). If you do neither, the experience is treated as completed.

4. PRICES AND PAYMENTS

Every menu shows a price per guest that includes our service fee and VAT. The price is made up of the Chef's own price, our service fee on top of it, a fast-track fee where the menu applies one to short-notice dates, and VAT on the total at the rate in force when you submit your request. Deposits, fast-track fees and the full price are shown before you book, and the amounts are fixed at the moment your request is submitted.

You pay the whole price through the App, by card, through our payment provider, Stripe: the deposit when the Chef accepts (if the menu requires one) and the balance before the event, as described in section 3. Chef Taste never stores your card details. Prices may be displayed in EUR, USD or GBP; conversions use our published rates and the amount charged is the amount shown at checkout.
