CHEF TASTE

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026

This policy explains what personal data the Chef Taste app collects, why, who it is shared with, and the rights you have under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Cypriot law. Chef Taste is the data controller for the data described here.

1. DATA WE COLLECT

  • Account details: name, surname, email address, phone number, password (stored only as a secure hash), your role (client or chef) and preferred display currency.
  • Client profile: your default address and city, the map pin for that address, and any dietary preferences or allergies you choose to save.
  • Chef profile: base city, years of experience, work history, menus, menu documents and availability.
  • Bookings: the menu, date, time, number of guests, event address and map pin, notes, dietary and allergy information at the time of booking, booking status, and the payment and refund status of any deposit.
  • Messages and reports: chat messages and photos exchanged with your chef, service reports and dispute evidence, reviews, and concierge chat transcripts.
  • Device data: a push-notification token for your device, and basic technical logs generated when the app talks to our servers.
  • Payments: card payments are processed by Stripe. We receive a payment reference, the amount, and the status of the payment or refund. We never receive or store your full card number.

2. WHY WE USE IT

  • To create and run your account and to carry out the bookings you request (performance of a contract).
  • To let the chef you book know where to cook, for how many guests, and which allergies to avoid (performance of a contract).
  • To take deposits, issue refunds and resolve disputes between clients and chefs (performance of a contract, legitimate interests).
  • To send notifications about your bookings, messages and account, including reminders the day before an event (performance of a contract). Push notifications can be turned off in your phone's settings.
  • To power the AI matchmaker and concierge features, which send your request text and, for the concierge, your surname to our AI provider to generate a reply (legitimate interests; you can simply not use these features).
  • To keep the platform safe, prevent fraud and enforce our terms (legitimate interests, legal obligations).

3. WHO SEES YOUR DATA

  • The chef you book sees your name, contact details, event address, guest count, notes, dietary and allergy information, and your messages, for that booking only. Clients see the chef's name, city, experience, menus and reviews.
  • Service providers that host and run the app on our behalf: Supabase (database, authentication, file storage and server functions, hosted in the European Union), Stripe (payments and refunds), Expo (push-notification delivery), OpenAI (AI matchmaker and concierge replies), and Google (maps and geocoding on Android). Each processes data only on our instructions.
  • Authorities where the law requires it.

We do not sell personal data and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

4. INTERNATIONAL TRANSFERS

Some providers (Stripe, Expo, OpenAI, Google) process data in the United States. Transfers rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or on European Commission standard contractual clauses.

5. HOW LONG WE KEEP IT

Account and booking data is kept while your account is active and for as long as needed afterwards to meet accounting and legal obligations (generally 6 years for payment records). Chat messages and dispute evidence are kept for the life of the related booking plus the period needed to handle disputes. Concierge transcripts are kept with your support history. When you delete your account, personal data that we are not legally required to retain is deleted or anonymised.

6. YOUR RIGHTS

You can access, correct, export or delete your personal data, object to or restrict certain processing, and withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. Name, phone, address and dietary details can be edited in the Profile screen. You can delete your account yourself in the app (Profile, then Delete Account), or follow the steps on our account deletion page. For anything else, contact us at the address below and we will respond within one month. You also have the right to complain to the Commissioner for Personal Data Protection of Cyprus (dataprotection.gov.cy).

7. SECURITY

Data is encrypted in transit, stored with per-user access rules so that clients and chefs only ever see the bookings they are part of, and photos are served through short-lived private links. No system is perfectly secure; if we become aware of a breach affecting you we will inform you as the law requires.

8. CHILDREN

The app is not intended for anyone under 18 and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. CHANGES

We may update this policy. Material changes will be announced in the app before they take effect.
