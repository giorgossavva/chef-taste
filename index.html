WHAT WE DO

A private chef, booked from your phone.

Chef Taste is a mobile application that connects clients with vetted professional chefs. Browse each chef's menus, background and reviews, pick a date, and the chef cooks and serves the experience at your villa, home or event. Chef Taste is the platform and payment intermediary; the culinary service itself is delivered by the independent chef you book.

HOW IT WORKS

1 · REQUESTChoose a menu, number of guests, date, time and address in the app. The chef has 72 hours to accept.
2 · DEPOSITOnce accepted, you have 24 hours to pay the deposit shown for that menu to secure the booking.
3 · EXPERIENCEThe chef arrives, cooks and serves. The remaining balance is settled as agreed for that menu.
4 · CONFIRMAfter the event you confirm the experience in the app, or report a problem, and rate your chef.

PRICING

Every menu shows a price per guest that already includes the Chef Taste service fee. Some chefs apply a fast-track fee for bookings made at short notice, and some require a deposit; both are displayed clearly on the menu before you request a booking, and the amounts are fixed at the moment you book. Prices are shown in your chosen currency (EUR, USD or GBP).

PAYMENTS

Deposits are paid by card inside the app. Card payments are processed by Stripe; Chef Taste never stores your card details. Charges appear on your statement as CHEF TASTE.

CANCELLATIONS & REFUNDS

  • Before the chef accepts: you may withdraw the request at any time at no cost.
  • Free cancellation window: each menu states a number of days before the event during which you can cancel for a full refund of your deposit. Refunds are returned to the original card within 5–10 business days.
  • Inside the window: cancelling less than the stated number of days before the event forfeits the deposit.
  • Chef cancels: your deposit is refunded in full, and our concierge helps you rebook with another chef.
  • Problems with the service: report it in the app within the confirmation window after the event. Our team reviews both sides and may issue a partial or full refund.

PRIVACY

We collect the details needed to run your bookings: name, contact details, event address, and any dietary or allergy information you choose to share. This information is shared only with the chef you book, and with our payment provider to process payments. We do not sell personal data. You can request deletion of your account and data at any time by contacting us. Read the full Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
